# WaveNova Partner Portal — User Guide

This guide walks you through everything in the Partner Portal so you can manage your station, track fundraising, and keep donors updated.

---

## 1. Logging In

1. Go to **wavenova.org**
2. Click **Partner Login** in the navigation bar
3. Sign in with your Google account (the same email that WaveNova registered you with)
4. You will be redirected to the Partner Portal automatically

> If you see "No partner org assigned to your account yet", contact hi@wavenova.org — your email needs to be linked to your station first.

---

## 2. Your Projects Home Screen

After logging in you land on **Your Projects**, a list of all stations assigned to your account.

Each project card shows:
- Station name and status (Operational, Just Launched, etc.)
- Location and category
- Total funds raised vs. goal across all active fundraising funds
- A progress bar showing overall funding percentage

Click **Manage →** on any project to open its management page.

---

## 3. Creating a New Project

If you need to register a new station, click **+ New Project** (top right of Your Projects).

Fill in the form:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Project Name** | The station's public display name (e.g. "Selong Belanak Station") |
| **Slug (URL)** | Auto-filled from the name. Only lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens. This becomes the page URL (e.g. `/projects/selong-belanak`) — it cannot be changed later |
| **Location** | City or area name shown to donors (e.g. "South Lombok") |
| **Category** | Choose "Sorting Stations" or "Waste Management" |
| **Project Image** | Upload a photo (JPG/PNG). Shown on the public project page |
| **Operating Since** | The year your station began operating (e.g. 2021) |
| **Description** | Optional brief description — you can add this later in the project editor |

Click **Create Project** when ready. The project appears in your list immediately.

---

## 4. Managing a Project

Click **Manage →** on a project to open its management page. This page has four sections.

---

### 4.1 Edit Project Content

This controls what donors see on your public project page at `wavenova.org/projects/[slug]`.

**About This Project**
Write a description of your station — what you do, who you work with, the impact you have. This is the main text donors read.

**Impact Metrics**
These are the teal tags donors see on your project card (e.g. "12,400 KG", "4 workers").

- To **add** a metric: type it in the input box and click **Add** (or press Enter)
- To **remove** a metric: click the **✕** on any existing tag

Keep metrics short and specific. Good examples:
- `12,400 KG collected`
- `6 workers employed`
- `Operating since 2021`

**Project Image**
Click the upload area to replace your station photo.

**Station Location**
This pins your station on the interactive map at `wavenova.org`. Search by typing your station's address or name in the search box — select from the autocomplete suggestions. Once selected, you'll see a confirmation message with the exact coordinates.

> This must be set accurately so donors can find your station on the map. Use Google Maps to look up your address first if unsure.

When you're done editing, click **Save Changes**. The button turns green ("Saved ✓") to confirm.

---

### 4.2 Fundraising Funds

Funds let you tell donors exactly what their money goes toward. Each fund has its own goal and progress bar.

**Creating a Fund**

Fill in the "New Fund" form at the bottom of this section:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Fund name** | What the money is for (e.g. "Facility Upgrade", "Equipment Purchase", "Monthly Operations") |
| **Goal ($)** | Target amount in USD |
| **Description** | Optional — more detail for donors |

Click **Create Fund**. It appears in the list immediately.

**Editing a Fund**

Click **Edit** next to any fund to change its name, goal, description, or status.

Fund statuses:
- **Active** — visible and accepting donations
- **Completed** — goal has been reached; mark it complete when fully funded
- **Archived** — hidden from donors; use for old or cancelled funds

Click **Save** to confirm changes.

> You can have multiple active funds at the same time. Donors choose which fund to support when they donate.

---

### 4.3 Allocating Donations

When a donor completes a bank transfer and WaveNova confirms it, the donation appears in the **Confirmed Donations** section.

Each entry shows:
- The donation amount (USD)
- Donor name (or "Anonymous")
- How many days ago it was received

Use the dropdown next to each donation to **allocate it to a fund**. This adds the amount to that fund's progress bar — the donor-facing page updates immediately.

> Always allocate donations promptly. Unallocated donations do not count toward any fund's progress.

If a donation was allocated to the wrong fund, simply select a different fund from the dropdown to reassign it.

---

### 4.4 Post a Station Update

Station updates appear in the activity feed visible to WaveNova internally. Use this to log meaningful milestones or collection events.

Write a short update in the text box (e.g. "Collected 340 KG this week" or "New sorting equipment installed") and click **Post Update**.

Posted updates appear in the **Recent Updates** list below the form, newest first.

> Post updates regularly — weekly or after major collection events. This creates a record of your station's activity over time.
